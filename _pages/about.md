Hi! My name is Sophia, I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Padua, and I completed my PhD at Yale University in March of 2025. My research specializes in experimental particle physics with a focus on the direct detection of dark matter and my [thesis](https://elischolar.library.yale.edu/gsas_dissertations/1650/) was on the combined search for the annual modulation of dark matter with the [COSINE-100](https://cosine.ibs.re.kr/) and [ANAIS-112](https://gifna.unizar.es/anais/) experiments which has ruled out the controversial dark matter [detection claim](https://jnpae.kinr.kyiv.ua/22.4/Articles_PDF/jnpae-2021-22-0329-Bernabei.pdf) by [DAMA/LIBRA](https://dama.web.roma2.infn.it/?page_id=64) that has persisted since 1997. These results can be seen in the selected publications below. 

The search for dark matter is still ongoing and this work requires the collaboration with researchers worldwide. My research has led me from the northeast United States, where I spent my formative years, to collaboration in South Korea with [COSINE-100](https://cosine.ibs.re.kr/), and to joining the research teams in Spain at the [CAPA](https://capa.unizar.es/) institute on [ANAIS-112](https://gifna.unizar.es/anais/) where I lived for 3 years. 

At [CAPA, Unizar](https://capa.unizar.es/) I am expanded the [ANAIS-112](https://gifna.unizar.es/anais/) physics program to search for solar axions through the annual modulation expected of Earth's distance from the Sun. Publication for this search is in progress, but you can view the pre-print at [arXiv:2607.27346](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27346) and the preliminary results which I presented as an [oral contribution](https://indico.capa.unizar.es/event/40/contributions/789/attachments/662/1176/HollickIDM2026.pdf) at IDM 2026. 

Additionally, I worked with the [RADES](https://rades.upct.es/home.html) collaboration which is a haloscope cavity experiment searching for axions with a variety of set-ups both at low and high frequencies. My work focused on optimizing the analysis and data storage protocols within the collaboration. Our proposal to use the CERN magnet for the low frequency search is available to view a pre-print on the arXiv here: [arXiv:2611.07806](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07806). 

I am now based in Padua, Italy where I have started work with the [QUAX](https://coldlab.lnf.infn.it/experiments/quax/) collaboration hosted at the INFN Padova Division laboratory. Our work includes a variety of axion haloscope cavity setups from linear amplifiers to the single microwave photon detector.
